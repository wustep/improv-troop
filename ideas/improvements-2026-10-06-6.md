## UI/UX & Design

### 1. Dynamics and solo cues on the sheet
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Plan dynamics and featured roles appear only in the Debug panel. The sheet draws tempo, feel, sections, rit. and chords (`render.ts`, `model.ts`).
- **Proposal.** Dynamics markings where they change; "solo"/"fill" labels per staff.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Vertical collisions.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/model.ts`, `src/sheet/render.ts`

### 2. Ghost notes and legato show on the sheet
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `addArticulations` maps only accent and staccato, so funk ghost notes read like backbeats.
- **Proposal.** Parenthesised ghost noteheads; tenuto for legato.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Width changes and layout tests.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/expand.ts`, `src/sheet/render.ts`

### 3. The spotlight and sparkles breathe with the soloist's phrasing
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Sparkles and the stage spotlight follow only the bar's role (`featured`), so a soloist resting between phrases glows at full strength while silent.
- **Proposal.** Dim both to a low level when the featured player has no note sounding, just played or about to play, with smoothing so choppy phrasing doesn't flicker.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Flicker; keep it smoothed.
- **Files.** `src/art/AnimalSprite.tsx`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`

### 4. Windups on vibes, bass and piano follow velocity
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The mallet lift, pluck flick and piano prep are fixed heights.
- **Proposal.** Scale them by the next onset's velocity.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Quiet parts may look too still.
- **Files.** `src/art/rigs/vibes.tsx`, `src/art/rigs/strings.tsx`, `src/art/rigs/piano.tsx`

### 5. The Debug Plan tab follows playback
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** There's no "now" row, and the Issues tab shows the latest run, not the loaded take.
- **Proposal.** Highlight the current row, click to seek, and filter issues by run.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Needs a score-to-run link.
- **Files.** `src/components/DebugPanel.tsx`

### 6. The tempo slider shows each style's home range
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Nothing shows a style's intended tempo range or default.
- **Proposal.** A highlighter band on the track, a default tick, and a "slow for Swing" hint.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Cross-browser range styling.
- **Files.** `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`

### 7. Opt out of forced dark mode
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** No `color-scheme` is declared, so auto-dark browsers may invert the hard-coded sheet ink and sprite colours.
- **Proposal.** `color-scheme: only light` plus metadata.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None.
- **Files.** `src/app/globals.css`, `src/app/layout.tsx`

## Music generation — LLM path

### 8. A bandmate's default reply no longer wipes the arrangement
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `improviser.ts` writes a bandmate's count-off `default` (e.g. `@walk`) over every accompanying bar. That erases the planner's shaped directives: the swing head in two, `@groove light`/`@groove peak`, shout `@riff`s, per-section melodic parts.
- **Proposal.** Treat the default as a swap for the player's usual directive only. Replace bars whose directive is that usual one, and leave shaped bars alone.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The model's taste counts for slightly less.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/pipeline.test.ts`

### 9. A motif moved into range keeps its text in step
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `validateMotif` shifts, folds and caps `m.notes` but leaves `m.text` (and `length`) as written. `motifBlock` sends the stale text to every later prompt, so the band is told the motif is two octaves off.
- **Proposal.** Rebuild `text` from the notes after any change.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Respelling may differ from what the model wrote.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/merge.test.ts`

### 10. A quality gate for the count-off motif
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Any 2–16-note motif is accepted, whether flat, leapy or off-style.
- **Proposal.** `motifQuality` checks plus harmony and a rhythm vocabulary in the count-off prompt.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Over-policing.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 11. A per-member directive vocabulary for replies (enum schema)
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Every player sees every directive, and the schema leaves `default` free text.
- **Proposal.** An `accompanimentVocab` enum in `replySchema`, plus a tailored prompt line.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Provider enum limits.
- **Files.** `src/ai/schemas.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 12. Cleaner say lines
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Lines use ids instead of names, carry notation, get cut mid-word, and repeat each other.
- **Proposal.** `cleanSay`, with at most one accompanist line per phrase.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Over-stripping.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/composer.ts`

### 13. Salvage gaps: truncated strings and other `bars` shapes
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Half-written strings are accepted, `finishReason` is dropped, and array or "bar N" forms are missed.
- **Proposal.** `truncatedPath`, `finishReason`, and a `barText` helper.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The array form is ambiguous.
- **Files.** `src/ai/json.ts`, `src/ai/llm.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 14. Call settings per role
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Temperatures are scattered, notation runs hottest, and thinking can eat the output cap.
- **Proposal.** A `ROLE_PARAMS` table with thinking headroom.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Samey solos.
- **Files.** `src/ai/models.ts`

### 15. A sloppy mode for the mock
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The mock is always well-formed, so pipeline tests never exercise repairs.
- **Proposal.** A seeded sloppy mode.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Brittle tests.
- **Files.** `src/ai/mock.ts`, `src/ai/pipeline.test.ts`

## Music generation — local band

### 16. Bass fifths and sevenths follow the chord's real root and quality
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `two()`, `bossa()`, `funk()` and the stride bass add a perfect fifth (and funk a fixed ♭7) whatever the chord. `funk`/`bossa` also build them on the slash bass, so G7/B gets F# and A. Over m7b5, dim and altered chords the fifth is wrong.
- **Proposal.** `fifthOf`/`seventhOf` helpers from the chord's root and tones; slash bass only for the "R" degree.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Output changes for existing seeds.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/bass.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`

### 17. Ghost notes aren't quietened twice
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Patterns already give ghosts low velocities, and the engine multiplies every ghost by 0.45 again, leaving funk bass ghosts near inaudible.
- **Proposal.** One ghost velocity rule in `feel.ts` with a floor, used by the engine.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The mix changes slightly.
- **Files.** `src/audio/feel.ts`, `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/audio/feel.test.ts`

### 18. Walking bass: turnarounds mid-register, enclosures, register by chorus
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Long one-way runs, single-note approaches only, and the same contour every chorus.
- **Proposal.** Direction changes, enclosures, a register bias per chorus.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** More chromaticism.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/bass.ts`

### 19. Funk comping repeats one riff per section
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `pickCell` hashes by bar, so the cell changes every bar while the bass riff holds.
- **Proposal.** Hash by section, plus a clavinet ghost layer.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Density.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`

### 20. New Orleans: refresh the riff per section; a tailgate trombone
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `mem.riff` is set once for the whole chart, and the low counter is just a thinned `line()`.
- **Proposal.** Riffs keyed by section; guide-tone smears.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Collisions with the lead.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/lines.ts`

### 21. Minimal: really additive, with real phasing
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The arp cell random-walks; `displace` drops the motif's tail.
- **Proposal.** An additive schedule, peer rotation, and wrapping displace.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Cell growth.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`, `src/music/motif.ts`

### 22. Motif transform fixes
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `retrograde` assumes a 0.95 duration factor (off-grid for text motifs); `rhythm` can index an empty cell.
- **Proposal.** Quantise reversed starts; guard the empty cell; accent the displaced note.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Small output change.
- **Files.** `src/music/motif.ts`
