## UI/UX & Design

### 1. Speech bubbles open toward the stage, per row
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `Stage.tsx` anchors bubbles with `i >= n / 2`, the position in the whole band, not the column in a wrapped row. On phones a right-column player in row 1 gets a left-anchored 15rem bubble that runs off the stage.
- **Proposal.** Anchor by column within the player's row (`col >= cols / 2`), and cap the bubble width to the row container.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Edge sprites at the narrowest slot can still clip slightly.
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`

### 2. The chart says "inking the chart…" forever if VexFlow fails
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `loadVexFlow()` rejection only logs; rejected promise is cached; rows stay `:empty` showing the loading placeholder forever.
- **Proposal.** `libError` state with "try again"; failed rows get their own message.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Retry needs the cached promise cleared.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`

### 3. On phones the director's card sits on top of the band
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Absolute `max-w-[16rem]` card at z-20 covers ~75% of a phone stage, including sprites and bubbles.
- **Proposal.** Container query: in-flow, unrotated, full width under 560px.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Adds height while the director writes.
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`

### 4. When stopped, the stage says what's loaded
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The stage header (`labelRef`/`chordRef`) is blank whenever stopped; title/key/tempo live only in the Transport status line.
- **Proposal.** Idle header shows title and "key · tempo"; trim the duplicate status text.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** rAF writes textContent directly; must restore idle text in the reset branch.
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 5. FormMap shows which bars the model actually wrote
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Only autopilot bars are marked; nothing distinguishes model-written notes, model directives and engine fallback, so the model's contribution is invisible.
- **Proposal.** Per-bar provenance tick in `FormMap.tsx` fed from merge/improviser bookkeeping.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Needs provenance threaded through Score.
- **Files.** `src/components/FormMap.tsx`, `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/music/types.ts`

### 6. One instrument picker open at a time, with Escape and focus return
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Each `MemberRow` owns `open`; several pickers open at once; Escape does nothing; focus is lost after choosing.
- **Proposal.** Lift `openId` to `ControlPanel`; Escape closes; focus returns to the trigger.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Small refactor of the most-used panel.
- **Files.** `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`

## Music generation — LLM path

### 9. Out-of-range model lines move as a whole, not note by note
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `finishPart` (`realize.ts`) and `validateMotif` (`merge.ts`) fold each out-of-range note by octaves on its own, and `validateBarText` doesn't check range at all, so a line that's slightly high gets an octave break mid-phrase and a high motif comes back with its contour turned inside out.
- **Proposal.** `fitRegister` in `merge.ts`: one octave shift for the whole bar or motif that puts the most notes in range (tie → smallest shift), recorded as a repair. `finishPart` stays as the per-note backstop.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Rewriting text could drop articulations; keep the original text when the shift is 0.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/merge.test.ts`

### 10. Trades actually trade: the drummer hears the horn's 2 bars first
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `featuredPhase` writes every featured player in a trade phrase at once, so the drummer can't answer bars written at the same moment.
- **Proposal.** In trade sections, call the featured players in bar order and give each one a LISTEN block.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Doubles the latency on trade phrases, so autopilot kicks in more often.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/prompts.ts`

### 11. Directives are checked against the instrument in every reply
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** The `okFor` table only guards the improviser count-off. Phrase replies and Composer's `mergePlan` accept any `@…` directive, so a piano can get `@walk` and a comping player can get `@solo`/`@motif`.
- **Proposal.** Move the check into `merge.ts` as `directiveFits` and apply it in `validateBarText` whenever a role is known, recording a repair. Mirror `bassCapable` so a cello on bass can still walk.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** If the table is too strict, legitimate cross-overs get rejected.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/composer.ts`, `src/ai/merge.test.ts`

### 12. Composer soloists hear the solo they're told to answer
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `arcNote` tells a soloist to "answer the last soloist", but all featured parts are written in parallel.
- **Proposal.** Sequence each handoff and pass along the last soloist's closing bars.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Slower Composer runs.
- **Files.** `src/ai/composer.ts`, `src/ai/prompts.ts`

### 13. Measure that the head states the motif and the solos quote it
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Nothing checks whether the motif survives model-written head bars, and the judge's coherence score is a guess.
- **Proposal.** `motifEcho` feature for the critic, falling back to `@motif` when the echo is weak.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Threshold needs tuning per style.
- **Files.** `src/ai/features.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/composer.ts`

### 14. Short phrase endings land on chord tones too
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `rehearse` only bends notes of ≥0.75 beat, so the arrival note before a rest goes unchecked.
- **Proposal.** Also treat the last note before a long rest as held.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Must keep intentional tensions.
- **Files.** `src/music/directives.ts`

### 15. The best-of-4 judge sees how much each candidate was repaired
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** A candidate that mostly fell back to the base plan can win on the engine's texture.
- **Proposal.** Add bars-kept and repair counts to `criticPrompt`, with a pick penalty.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Repair counts partly reflect how strict the parser is.
- **Files.** `src/ai/composer.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`

### 16. Repairs that respect bar arithmetic
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Multi-bar cells are cut to their first bar, short bars are padded with rest, and overfull bars are clipped.
- **Proposal.** Spill extra segments into missing bars, extend the last note of a short bar, and rescale bars written at exactly double length.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Tuplet grid edge cases.
- **Files.** `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/music/notation.ts`

## Music generation — local band

### 17. Drum fills come in more than one shape
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `fill()` in `patterns/drums.ts` uses no randomness: every fill is the same descending snare→tom run, repeated 4–8 times per take, which is the most audible repetition in the band.
- **Proposal.** A small fill vocabulary per style, chosen with `ctx.rng`, never repeating the previous shape. Section ends get the bigger shapes.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** A busier fill could crowd a soloist's ending, so the small shapes stay at 1 beat or less.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/drums.ts`, `src/music/context.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`

### 18. Swing heads in two, solos walking
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Swing walks from bar 1 to the end (`@two` exists only in the intro, which local takes never generate), so the solos get no lift.
- **Proposal.** Head bass `@two` on heads of 8 bars or more, walking into the last head bar. Solos and the out head walk.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Short charts would be mostly two-feel, so the change is gated by head length.
- **Files.** `src/music/styles.ts`, `src/music/planner.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`

### 19. Bossa bass and comping anticipate the next chord
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The docstring promises a push on &4, but the code plays the current fifth.
- **Proposal.** Play the next root on 3.5 when the chord changes, and mark the comp anticipation.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The kick follows the bass.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/bass.ts`, `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`

### 20. Funk and bossa grooves turn around at phrase ends
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The funk riff loops identically through the whole section.
- **Proposal.** A turnaround cell on phrase-end bars only.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Kick-count limit.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/bass.ts`

### 21. A dynamic arc across the whole chart
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `arcDynamic` has no-op branches; every solo peaks at f.
- **Proposal.** Solo-order-aware arc: bring it down, then build to ff on the last solo.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Energy also drives density.
- **Files.** `src/music/planner.ts`

### 22. Hairpins: velocity ramps into a dynamic change
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Dynamics step at barlines.
- **Proposal.** Ramp velocity in the bar before a change, for parts that aren't featured.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Downbeat crash interaction.
- **Files.** `src/music/realize.ts`

### 23. Baroque bass avoids parallel fifths and octaves with the melody
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `baroque()` ignores the written melody.
- **Proposal.** A strong-beat parallel check with a deterministic alternative.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Can fight the goal-directed walk at cadences.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/bass.ts`

### 24. Band talk for handoffs and shout choruses
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** An `@answer` opener gets the generic line, and shout riffs go unnarrated.
- **Proposal.** ANSWER lines naming the previous soloist, plus a riff call.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None to the music.
- **Files.** `src/music/narrate.ts`
