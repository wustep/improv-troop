## Technical

### 1. Pace TalkGate across the whole jam, and keep talk in bar order
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `src/ai/talk.ts` only limits talk per phrase, so the first phrases fill up. The count-off lines push later lines into "filler". Phrase pi+1's featured line can arrive before phrase pi's, and the cooldown then drops it. `say()` appends lines out of bar order.
- **Proposal.** A budget of lines for the whole jam, paced by phrase. A symmetric cooldown. Count-off lines left out of the filler window. `insertByBar` in both the pipeline and the store.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Fewer lines in long jams.
- **Files.** `src/ai/talk.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 2. A reload during a jam brings back the jam, not a sketch with stock talk
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `generate()` saves the new seed up front. The take is only saved in `finish`. On reload, `sketch()` rebuilds a local take from that seed, with `narrateLocal`'s stock talk (`store.ts:147,230,310,357`).
- **Proposal.** Save `onScore` snapshots, throttled, as a partial take. On hydrate, restore it with its real chat and mark it as cut short.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** localStorage writes during the jam.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`, `src/state/takes.ts`

### 3. Decide which bars need a recount after local repair
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ✅ accepted (not shipped: see above)
- **Problem.** `composer.ts:373-377` flags bars from the raw `parseNotes`, before spelling, fitting beat groups or squeezing, so it pays for recounts the local repair would have done.
- **Proposal.** `needsRecount()` runs the same steps as `validateBarText`.
- **Files.** `src/ai/composer.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`

### 4. A dropped duration in model text is not carried forward
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted (not shipped: see above)
- **Problem.** `parseNotes` reuses the previous duration, so a run of 16ths with one `/16` missing gets misread.
- **Proposal.** When a bar doesn't fit, try reading the bare tokens as the bar's most common duration, and keep that reading only if it fits exactly.
- **Files.** `src/music/notation.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`
