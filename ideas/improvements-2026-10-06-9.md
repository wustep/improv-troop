## Music generation — LLM path

### 1. Autopilot leaves the phrase's model calls running
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** When `ensureReady` vamps through a phrase (`improviser.ts:141-156`), that phrase's featured and band calls keep going. They're only discarded on arrival ("arrived after the band had already vamped", `improviser.ts:369, 462`). Meanwhile each holds one of the six call slots (`llm.ts:33-60`) and may retry up to three times, which delays the next live phrase (making more autopilot likely) and spends the user's key.
- **Proposal.** Give each phrase an AbortController joined to the run's signal. Abort it when autopilot plays the phrase. A call cut short that way is dropped quietly, while a cancelled run still throws.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** A phrase abort must never be read as the run being cancelled.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/pipeline.test.ts`

### 2. A failed count-off ends the whole jam
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** If the leader's count-off call fails after its retries (a 5xx, a timeout), the run throws "couldn't call the tune" (`improviser.ts:227-230`). The local motif and plan are already built (`:66-67`), and an empty reply already carries on fine.
- **Proposal.** Rethrow only for a cancel or a bad key (401/403). Otherwise the leader counts off the engine's own motif and plan, a note says so, and the bandmates still reply.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The jam sounds less like the leader's idea, which the note says.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/pipeline.test.ts`

### 3. After falling behind, the band rarely gets back in
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Once autopilot overtakes a phrase, the loop starts the next featured phase only afterwards, so two serial rounds have to fit inside one phrase (`improviser.ts:503-508`).
- **Proposal.** Skip ahead to the first phrase that can still be ready in time, and run soloist and band in parallel for catch-up phrases.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Catch-up phrases listen less closely.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 4. Best-of-4 drafts aren't varied, and hollow drafts still compete
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The same prompt goes out four times, and the temperature steps are dropped for the default Sonnet (`composer.ts:176, 190`, `models.ts:20`). A draft with no usable bars still becomes a candidate (`composer.ts:213`).
- **Proposal.** Give each draft an angle line, and drop candidates that cover under half the bars.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Angles could push drafts outside the style.
- **Files.** `src/ai/composer.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`

### 5. Band talk lines arrive out of bar order
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `onChat` appends in arrival order (`store.ts:327`), so pipelined replies interleave as "bar 5 / bar 2 / bar 5".
- **Proposal.** Insert jam lines in bar order.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Low.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`

## Music generation — local band

### 6. Accents are boosted twice
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Model notes marked `>` get `vel*1.2` (`directives.ts:124`), the button ending gets `*1.1` (`directives.ts:271`), and stop-time hits get `*1.15` (`realize.ts:105`), all also marked `accent`. The audio engine then boosts every accent again (`vel*1.12+0.08`, `engine.ts:942`). At mf an accent goes 0.67 → 0.80 → 0.98 and clamps to full. At p it jumps 55%. Every accent flattens to maximum.
- **Proposal.** Accent in one place: the engine, which the drum sample choice also keys on. Drop the pre-boosts.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Stop-time and button hits get slightly softer before the engine's boost.
- **Files.** `src/music/directives.ts`, `src/music/realize.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`

### 7. The out head replays a melody over cadence chords that changed
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `melodySource` (`planner.ts:99-111`) compares only each bar's first chord. A free chart's cadence bar (`form.ts:157-161`) adds a V7 on beat 3 of a bar that copies a head bar ("Dm7 G7" against "Dm7"), so `@head` replays notes written over Dm7 on top of G7.
- **Proposal.** Require the whole chord list (symbols and beats) to match. Otherwise the leader plays the motif-based cadence line the planner already has.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The last bars of an out head are a little less literal.
- **Files.** `src/music/planner.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`

### 8. The free-chart cadence stacks two dominants on one root
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The `first === v7` string check (`form.ts:157-161`) lets "G G7" and "G7b9 G7" through. Ambient gets a hard G7.
- **Proposal.** Compare roots, and use a per-style cadence chord.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Seeded charts change.
- **Files.** `src/music/form.ts`

### 9. Fade and cadence endings peak during the ritardando
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The last two out bars get the `peak` texture (`planner.ts:73`), which are the rit bars of the bossa fade and the baroque cadence.
- **Proposal.** Use tutti or sparse with a falling dynamic for those endings.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Swing and New Orleans keep their peak.
- **Files.** `src/music/planner.ts`

### 10. Ensemble pitch moves skip the release-at-the-change check
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Passes 2–4 move pitches after pass 1's release check (`ensemble.ts:118-126, 197-262`).
- **Proposal.** Run `releaseAtChange` on every moved note.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Slightly shorter pads.
- **Files.** `src/music/ensemble.ts`

### 11. Reharm reads the next bar before the turnaround replaces it
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The turnaround is written after `reharmonize` (`form.ts:149, 154`).
- **Proposal.** Write turnaround and cadence bars first.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Seeded charts change.
- **Files.** `src/music/form.ts`

### 12. Uneven solo lengths
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The leftover goes to the last soloist: 8/8/16 over 32 bars (`form.ts:85-96`).
- **Proposal.** Spread the remainder in unit steps.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Layouts change.
- **Files.** `src/music/form.ts`

## UI/UX & Design

### 13. An instrument that fails to load looks ready, and the load bar runs backwards
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `Transport.tsx:32` leaves errored loads out of `loading`, so "N of M ready" counts a failed instrument as ready, and the player mimes in silence. Only the debug Audio tab shows ✗. `loadPct` averages over the instruments still loading, so it jumps back when a fast one finishes and drops out.
- **Proposal.** Compute progress over every load, counting ready or failed as complete. When something failed, show "✗ Rusty's trumpet didn't load" in ink with a "try again" action that re-prepares (the engine already retries failed entries), using the shipped error pattern.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Retrying repeatedly is fine because the engine gates on `failedAt`.
- **Files.** `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 14. Sheet text shrinks to about 7px on phones
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Systems at least 720 wide are CSS-zoomed to as little as 0.6 (`SheetMusic.tsx:129-138`, `model.ts:23`). Labels at 12–13px render at about 7px (`render.ts:423-481`).
- **Proposal.** Pass the zoom into `renderRow` and floor the effective text sizes.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Chord text collides with stems.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/render.ts`, `SheetMusic.tsx`, `model.ts`

### 15. The sheet's hard-coded colours
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `INK_SOFT = "#6a6474"` and a literal highlighter rgba (`render.ts:11, 590`). The halo is stroked with the canvas colour on the lighter sheet (`render.ts:548`).
- **Proposal.** Resolve tokens once with `getComputedStyle`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Cached SVGs keep the old colours until reload.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/render.ts`

### 17. Share links can seat 12 players; model takes can't be shared, without a word
