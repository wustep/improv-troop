## Technical

### 1. The tempo slider restarts the transport on every input event
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** The `ControlPanel` tempo range calls `setSettings({ tempo })` on every `onChange`. `setSettings` in `src/state/store.ts` then persists to localStorage, builds a new `Score`, and while playing calls `troopAudio.play(next, { countIn: false })`. That halts every instrument, re-awaits `prepare`, and rebuilds `FrameComputer` and the sheet model. Dragging the slider stutters the audio about 60 times a second.
- **Proposal.** Add `TroopAudio.setTempo(bpm)`, which re-anchors the clock so the current beat stays put without halting. The store calls it instead of `play()` and only persists once the drag ends.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Re-anchoring must handle `rit`. Notes already scheduled inside the lookahead keep the old timing.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/state/store.ts`, `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`

### 2. The live-improv watchdog runs on rAF, so it stops in background tabs
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** The "playback caught up with the band" check in `play()` (`store.ts`) runs on `requestAnimationFrame`, which browsers pause in hidden tabs. The audio scheduler keeps playing (`LOOKAHEAD_HIDDEN_SEC`), so the transport runs past `readyBars` with no autopilot fill. The loop also runs during every playback, even when there's no live improv.
- **Proposal.** Use a `setInterval` (about 100 ms) and start it only while `improv` exists.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Hidden-tab timers are throttled to about 1 s, which still fits inside the 1.5 s hidden lookahead.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`

### 3. The sheet's tempo marking goes stale after a tempo change
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Row 0 draws `♩ = tempo` (`render.ts`), but `signature()` in `SheetMusic.tsx` leaves out tempo, so the row cache hands back the old SVG.
- **Proposal.** Include tempo in the signature, or draw the marking outside the cached SVG.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None significant.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`, `src/sheet/render.ts`

### 4. Validate persisted settings on hydrate
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `load()` returns raw `JSON.parse` output that is spread over the defaults. A bad `style` or a non-array `soloists` can crash first paint.
- **Proposal.** Pull out `sanitizeSettings` from `decodeShare` and use it in both places.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Must not wipe the apiKey.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`, `src/state/share.ts`

### 5. One shared frame clock instead of 6+ rAF loops
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Stage, each AnimalSprite, FormMap, SheetMusic and the watchdog each run their own rAF loop and read `getBeat()` separately.
- **Proposal.** Add `src/audio/clock.ts`: one rAF with subscribers.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Regressions in sprite idle motion.
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/art/AnimalSprite.tsx`, `src/components/FormMap.tsx`, `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`

## UI/UX & Design

### 6. Keep Play within reach on phones
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Below `lg`, the long ControlPanel sits between Transport and the chart, so scrolling down loses Play/Stop.
- **Proposal.** A sticky compact transport once the inline one scrolls out of view.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Covers the chart. Needs safe-area padding.
- **Files.** `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 7. Honor reduced motion everywhere, not just bubbles
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Only the bubble and thinking dots respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Smooth follow-scroll, the chevron, the press translate and the fades all still move.
- **Proposal.** Zero the motion tokens in the media query and use `behavior: "auto"` for the sheet scroll.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Don't remove the "who's playing" sprite motion.
- **Files.** `src/app/globals.css`, `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`

### 8. Stop hiding affordances behind hover
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The take-delete "×" and the "tap to mute" hint only appear on hover, so touch and keyboard users never see them.
- **Proposal.** Show them on `(hover: none)` and on focus-within. Add undo for delete.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Accidental deletes on mobile.
- **Files.** `src/components/Transport.tsx`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`

### 9. Cut screen-reader noise in the transport status line
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The whole status line is `aria-live`, so every status tick is announced. The alert is nested inside it, and the share button's name changes under focus.
- **Proposal.** A hidden polite region that announces milestones only, with a fixed label on the share button.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Needs a screen-reader pass.
- **Files.** `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 10. Visible focus ring on the sheet scroller
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The inline `outline: "none"` overrides the theme's `:focus-visible` ring (`SheetMusic.tsx`).
- **Proposal.** Remove it and use a negative offset if the ring gets clipped.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`

## Product Features

### 11. A landing note for share links
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Opening a share link quietly loads the take. Nothing says "someone sent you this" or invites a press of Play (autoplay is blocked), and the visitor's own band in the panel doesn't match the stage.
- **Proposal.** A sticky note in the IntroNote style: "Someone sent you a take: … played by …", with "▶ play it" and "jam with this band", which adopts the band and settings. Dismissed on first play.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Must never overwrite the visitor's band unless they ask.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`

### 12. Record and download audio
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** There's no way to keep what you heard.
- **Proposal.** Tap the compressor into a MediaStreamDestination and record with MediaRecorder until `onEnded`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Real-time only. Formats differ by browser.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 13. "Surprise me": random band and style
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** New visitors have to choose everything before hearing anything different.
- **Proposal.** A 🎲 that picks a sensible seeded band and a style or standard, then sketches.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Overwrites the band, so it needs an undo.
- **Files.** `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 14. Solo-listen one player
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Hearing one part alone means muting everyone else one by one.
- **Proposal.** A `soloListen(id)` toggle on the name tag.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Gesture conflicts on touch.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`

### 15. Reopen the last take on reload
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `hydrate()` always shows the sketch, even when the user was on a saved take.
- **Proposal.** Remember the selected take id and restore it.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Can be confusing after settings change.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`

## DevEx

### 16. Unit tests for the model-reply salvage layer (json.ts, merge.ts)
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `extractJson` (fences, trailing commas, truncated replies), `resolveMember`, `validateBarText`, `validateMotif`, `mergePlan` and `enforceSlots` are only covered through `fakeModel` happy paths.
- **Proposal.** Table-driven tests built from realistic messy replies, asserting the cleaned output and the repairs.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Match repair messages on substrings.
- **Files.** `src/ai/json.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`, new tests

### 17. Notation round-trip tests
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `notesToText`/`parseNotes` and `drumsToGrid`/`parseDrumGrid` are inverses that models read and write. Only 3 hand-picked parse cases test them.
- **Proposal.** Round-trip every bar of `generateLocal` output across styles × seeds × meters.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Might turn up real lossy cases, which then need fixing or allow-listing.
- **Files.** `src/music/notation.ts`, new `src/music/notation.test.ts`

### 18. Run `next build` in CI
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** CI only typechecks, lints and tests, so prerender or bundling failures slip through.
- **Proposal.** Add a build step with `.next/cache` cached.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Adds 1–2 minutes.
- **Files.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml`

### 19. Store persistence tests
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `load`/`save`/`loadTakes`/`saveTakes` and the quota fallback have no tests.
- **Proposal.** Stubbed-localStorage tests covering corrupt JSON and unknown values.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The module-level zustand store needs a reset between tests.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`

### 20. A pure track/cursor module for the audio scheduler, with tests
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The `updateScore` cursor resume, the loop-wrap reset and `lowerBound`/`upperBound` in `engine.ts` keep live score swaps from double-playing, and none of them are tested.
- **Proposal.** Extract them into `src/audio/tracks.ts` and test them.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** A refactor in a hot path.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`
