## UI/UX & Design

### 1. The stage shows the chord coming next, not just the one sounding
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** While playing, `chordRef` in `Stage.tsx` shows only the current chord, though the next one is known (`score.frame.chords`). Musicians read ahead, and so does anyone trying to follow the changes.
- **Proposal.** After the current chord, a small ink-soft "→ next" chord, written from the same ref-driven rAF loop, and hidden on narrow stages.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Crowding at `text-xl`; must stay ref-driven (no per-frame renders).
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`
- **Outcome.** Shipped in b79ff26 and verified live at 375px ("Bb6 → Eb13"). Also fixed a bug found along the way: the label and chord stayed on stage after a stop.

### 2. Number keys mute players
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Muting needs the pointer, even though Space already has a play shortcut.
- **Proposal.** Keys 1–N toggle mute, using the same "not typing" guard as Space.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Could clash with future shortcuts.
- **Files.** `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/components/Transport.tsx`

### 3. BandTalk's empty state links to the mode switch
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `BandTalk.tsx` explains both modes but offers no way to change mode from there.
- **Proposal.** A text-action that focuses the mode chips.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Cross-component focus wiring.
- **Files.** `src/components/BandTalk.tsx`, `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`

## Music generation — LLM path

### 4. Accompanists who write notes get the harmony and the soloist's gaps
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `accompanimentPhase` invites written fills but sends no `harmonyBlock`, and doesn't say where the soloist rests.
- **Proposal.** A chord-tone harmony line and a `GAPS:` line for melodic and chordal accompanists.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Adds tokens to the widest fan-out.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/prompts.ts`

### 5. Featured players see the next chord, so phrases land on it
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `harmonyBlock(frame, plan, myBars)` lists only the bars being written, so the model can't aim a phrase ending at the chord that follows its last bar.
- **Proposal.** `harmonyBlock` takes an optional next bar and appends "then bar N: X (aim your last note at one of these: …)". Improviser featured calls and Composer `partsPrompt` pass it.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** ~30 tokens per call.
- **Files.** `src/ai/prompts.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/composer.ts`
- **Outcome.** Shipped in 3b3e319 (inside `harmonyBlock`, so both callers get it), with a new `prompts.test.ts`.

### 6. The critic's own scores decide, and candidates are judged fairly
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `runComposer` takes the judge's `best` over the weighted scores it just gave. Candidates are always shown in order 1..N, so position bias decides. Each candidate is realized with `seed + index`, so the features partly measure seed noise.
- **Proposal.** Pick by score, with `best` only breaking ties. Ignore out-of-range indexes and clamp scores. Show candidates in a seeded shuffle. Realize every candidate with the same seed.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Small.
- **Files.** `src/ai/composer.ts`
- **Outcome.** Shipped in 450b27a, with `pickCandidate` and tests.

### 7. Featured players get a solo vocabulary; unknown directives are rejected
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The featured prompt never mentions `@answer`, and typos like `@blues` pass validation silently.
- **Proposal.** A featured vocabulary line, plus a `KNOWN_DIRECTIVES` check in `validateBarText`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The list can drift from `realizeRaw`.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`, `src/ai/merge.ts`, `src/music/directives.ts`

### 8. Live soloists see only the nearby part of the chart
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Every featured call resends the whole chart.
- **Proposal.** A windowed `chartBlock` around the phrase, keeping the Form line.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Less sense of the big form.
- **Files.** `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 9. Ties hold across the barline
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `G4/2~` at the end of a bar re-strikes in the next bar.
- **Proposal.** A post-realize merge pass for ties that cross the barline.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Interaction with `rehearse`.
- **Files.** `src/music/realize.ts`, `src/music/directives.ts`

### 10. The critic reads pitch names, not MIDI numbers
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `textureFeatures` reports `register 55–79`.
- **Proposal.** Use `pitchName`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** None.
- **Files.** `src/ai/features.ts`

## Music generation — local band

### 11. Standards end on the tonic, not on the turnaround
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Free charts force the last bar to the tonic (`form.ts`), but standards copy the form's last bar as is. A take of Blue Bossa ends on its turnaround (`Dm7b5 G7`), and a blues on `Gm7 C7`. `endChord` then holds that chord for the whole fermata.
- **Proposal.** In `buildFrame`'s standards branch, make the last bar the tonic when it isn't already, and end the bar before it on V7 when that bar was a turnaround.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** `melodySource` replay of the penultimate bar; blues tonic colour.
- **Files.** `src/music/form.ts`, `src/music/engine.test.ts`
- **Outcome.** Confirmed first: 6 of 11 standards ended on a turnaround. Shipped in a280620; every standard at every length now ends on its tonic.

### 12. Ambient pads float through held harmony
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S–M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Ambient holds chords for two bars, but `pad()` re-attacks the same voicing every downbeat. `shimmer()` picks bell pitches uniformly at random, with no line.
- **Proposal.** Hold the pad through an unchanged chord instead of re-striking it, and weight bells toward 4ths and 5ths from the last bell.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Incremental realize; ensemble release-at-change.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`, `src/music/context.ts`
- **Outcome.** Shipped in 8e22330. Pads held in 44 of 45 eligible bars (from 0); bell 4ths and 5ths up from 29% to 51%.

### 13. Solos save their top note and don't repeat their peaks
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Phrase apexes repeat, and the ceiling is reachable early.
- **Proposal.** Remember recent peaks, and open the ceiling with the arc.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Pitch-echo sequences; short trades.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/phrase.ts`, `src/music/context.ts`

### 14. Jazz-waltz comping vocabulary
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** 3/4 swing comping is a fixed oom-pah-pah that never listens.
- **Proposal.** Waltz cells routed through the same `listen()` and mid-bar-change logic as 4/4.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Anticipation voicing.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`

### 15. Comp voicings respect low-interval limits and lead the top voice
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Rootless voicings can sit muddy around G2–C3, and nothing weights the top voice.
- **Proposal.** A low-interval-limit penalty plus a top-voice motion cost in `voiceChord`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** More shell-voicing fallbacks.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/voicing.ts`

### 16. Baroque prelude planned under the tune, with more than one figure
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The prelude arpeggio is hard-coded to 60–79 with one figure, and the ensemble pass repairs its clashes one note at a time.
- **Proposal.** Use `voiceRange`, and pick a figure per phrase.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Inner-voice collision.
- **Files.** `src/music/patterns/comp.ts`

### 17. A blues out-chorus is a whole chorus
- **Impact:** ⚪ low · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** With two blues choruses the out is only the last four bars.
- **Proposal.** Give the out a full chorus when the form is a blues.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Less room for solos.
- **Files.** `src/music/form.ts`
