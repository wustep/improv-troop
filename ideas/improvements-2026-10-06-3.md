## Technical

### 1. Instruments are never disposed, and a failed instrument is never retried
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** `getEntry` (`src/audio/engine.ts`) caches every smplr instance under `${instrument}:${pack}#k` forever, and `assignEntries` never prunes `memberEntry`/`memberPan`. Swapping instruments, switching the piano pack or opening share links with other bands piles up decoded sample buffers (pianos are many MB), and `halt()` stops every entry ever made. An entry with `failed: true` is handed back on every later `prepare`, so one network blip silences that player until reload.
- **Proposal.** After `prepare`, dispose entries no member uses, but only while stopped, and prune the member maps. Retry failed entries on the next `prepare`, with a short backoff.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Must never dispose something that's sounding. Switching back re-downloads, which the HTTP cache softens.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`

### 2. A tempo change during live improv is undone by the next phrase
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `startImproviser` captures `frame` once, so `snapshot()` returns the starting tempo. `onScore`/`finish` in `store.ts` then put the old tempo back into `current` and the saved take, while the audio keeps the new one.
- **Proposal.** Overlay `settings.tempo` onto each incoming score in `onScore`/`finish`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Swing depends on tempo.
- **Files.** `src/state/store.ts`, `src/ai/improviser.ts`

### 3. setTempo follow-ups: loading, count-in, pocket and pizz mute
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** While loading or counting in, `setTempo` returns false and the store restarts playback on every slider pixel, dropping the count-in and leaking an 8s timeout each time. `setTempo` also keeps the old-tempo `pocket` closures. Separately, `setMute` doesn't stop the `|pizz` voice.
- **Proposal.** Accept the tempo while loading, rebuild the clicks during count-in, rebuild pockets, and stop the pizz entry on mute.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Token logic in `startWhenReady`.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 4. The UI is stuck on "playing" if the engine fails to start
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `store.play` sets `playing: true` right away. When `startWhenReady` throws, the engine halts without firing ended, so the UI and watchdog keep running. Stop pressed during `unlock()` is overridden.
- **Proposal.** Emit ended or an error on a failed start, and add a play-token check in `store.play`.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Minimal.
- **Files.** `src/audio/engine.ts`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 5. A tempo drag rebuilds the sheet model, the VexFlow measurements and FrameComputer
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** Every tempo change makes a new Score object, so `buildModel`, `measureChart` and `new FrameComputer` all rerun, though only tempo changed.
- **Proposal.** Memoize on content plus tempo separately, and pass tempo to FrameComputer as a parameter.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Pocket scaling depends on tempo.
- **Files.** `src/sheet/SheetMusic.tsx`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/components/stage/frames.ts`

## UI/UX & Design

### 6. Make "add a gateway key" a real path, not a tooltip
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** The only routes to turning on the AI are text. The intro note says "Add a gateway key" (`Stage.tsx`), the no-key hint is a `title` tooltip on the go button that touch devices never show (`Transport.tsx`), and the key-rejected error says "check it in Brains & sounds", a `<details>` that starts closed at the bottom of a long panel (`ControlPanel.tsx`). The key field doesn't say where to get a key or confirm that one was saved.
- **Proposal.** A store-level `openBrains()` opens the details, scrolls them into view and focuses the key input. Wire it into the intro note, a visible "add a key" text-action next to the go button when there's no key, and the key-error actions. Under the input, add a link to get a key and confirm when one is saved.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The panel has its own scroll container at `lg`. Never move focus on page load.
- **Files.** `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`, `src/components/Transport.tsx`, `src/components/Stage.tsx`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 7. The settings panel stops matching the stage when replaying a take
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** `selectTake` swaps `current` but not `settings`/`members`. The panel shows today's choices, and touching any of them quietly discards the take.
- **Proposal.** A "Listening to take #3…" line in the panel with "use these settings" and "back to my sketch".
- **Risks / trade-offs.** Deciding what counts as "different".
- **Files.** `src/components/ControlPanel.tsx`, `src/state/store.ts`

### 9. One vocabulary for "sketch" and "take"

- **Problem.** The status line mixes "sketch", "take" and "sketch take", and first-run copy never explains the difference.

### 10. Band talk follows the playhead
- **Impact:** 🟡 med · **Effort:** M · **Status:** ❌ rejected
- **Problem.** The list always jumps to the bottom and never marks the line being played. Its glyph avatars are read aloud.
- **Proposal.** Highlight lines for the current bar, auto-scroll only when already near the bottom, make bar dividers seek, and hide the glyphs from screen readers.

- **Files.** `src/components/BandTalk.tsx`

## Product Features

### 11. The local band plays in character
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Every animal has a `persona` (`instruments.ts`: Mochi "leaves space, answers phrases sideways", Clover "loves a sequence"), but only model prompts read it. The local engine, which is what everyone hears without a key, picks each soloist's opener from one uniform list (`SOLO_OPENERS`, `planner.ts`), so every animal solos alike.
- **Proposal.** Give each animal a small `taste`: weights over openers, plus a bias toward space or density. `planLocal` picks with it. Band talk follows automatically, since it reads the plan.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** The same seed now plays differently from before, so older local share links replay with today's band (the share format already says the engine isn't versioned). Keep biases mild so the style still leads.
- **Files.** `src/music/instruments.ts`, `src/music/planner.ts`, tests

## DevEx

### 16. A lint rule that keeps src/music deterministic
- **Impact:** 🔴 high · **Effort:** S · **Status:** ✅ accepted
- **Problem.** Share links and "same seed = same take" depend on the engine never reaching for `Math.random`/`Date.now`, and nothing enforces that. A stray call in `patterns/*.ts` would silently break replay.
- **Proposal.** In `eslint.config.mjs`, scoped to `src/music/**`, ban `Math.random`, `Date.now` and `crypto.getRandomValues`, with a message pointing to `Rng`. The take id/timestamp in `local.ts` get a targeted disable with a reason. Timing via `performance.now` is allowed, since it doesn't affect output.
- **Risks / trade-offs.** A couple of justified disables.
- **Files.** `eslint.config.mjs`, `src/music/local.ts`
